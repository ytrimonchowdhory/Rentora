Trust & Safety

Your safety is our priority. Here's how we keep the Rentora community secure.

Verified Listings

All equipment listings are reviewed before being published. We verify owner details to ensure authenticity and prevent fraudulent listings.

Secure Data Handling

Your personal information is encrypted and securely stored. We never share your data with third parties without your consent. All form submissions use CSRF protection.

Community Guidelines

All users must follow our community guidelines. Respectful communication, accurate descriptions, and timely returns are expected from everyone.

Terms of Service

By using Rentora, you agree to our terms. Renters are responsible for returning equipment in good condition. Owners must provide accurate descriptions of their items.

Important Notice

Rentora is a marketplace platform that connects equipment owners with renters. We facilitate connections but are not directly responsible for the condition of equipment or the conduct of users. Always inspect equipment before renting and communicate clearly with the other party.

If you encounter any issues, please contact us immediately.

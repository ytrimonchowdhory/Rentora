How Rentora Works

Two simple paths — one for renters, one for equipment owners.

For Renters (I Want to Rent)

1

Browse or Request

Search through available equipment listings, or submit a request for specific equipment you need. No account required.

2

Get Matched

Our system connects you with equipment owners who have what you need. We verify listings for quality and safety.

3

Rent & Return

Pick up the equipment from the owner, use it for your project, and return it when done. Simple and hassle-free.

For Owners (I Want to List)

1

List Your Equipment

Fill out a simple form with equipment details, photos, and your daily rate. Your email becomes your unique ID.

2

Get Rental Requests

When someone wants to rent your equipment, we notify you immediately via email with their details.

3

Earn Money

Set your own rates and earn money from equipment that would otherwise sit idle. It's that simple.
